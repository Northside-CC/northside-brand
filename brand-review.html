Usage

Clear space and size

  • Keep half the logo's height clear on every side.
  • Never show the full logo smaller than 1 inch wide.
  • Use the full logo in most contexts.

Don't

    Sub-brands

    A Branded House

    We are a "branded house," not a "house of brands." Ministries do not exist apart from Northside, so every ministry logo follows the same structure as the main Northside Christian Church logo.

    The rule

    • Ministry logos follow the Northside pattern.
    • Do not create a separate logo for a ministry or event team.

    The exceptions

    • Kidside has its own brand guidelines.
    • NSM (Northside Student Ministry) has its own brand guidelines.

    Sub-brand logo files are not in this kit yet.

      Look and Feel

      The supporting pieces that make a design feel like Northside.

      Northside Waves

      • A line-based graphic used in the corners of a piece to balance it and add rhythm.

      Noise

      • Add 12% to 15% noise (uniform, monochrome) to some photos and backgrounds for contrast.

      Photo containers

      • Circular image frames may be used in advertising and internal pieces.

      Textures

      • Textured paper, scratches, and torn edges are fine in moderation. Legibility comes first.
